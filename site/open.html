The open pattern

A pattern before it is a product.

Anyone can use the sovereign agentic website pattern without paying us, the way anyone can install WordPress. That is the point: a site you own should not depend on us existing.

Go to sovereignwebsite.org SovereignWebsite on GitHub

What the pattern is

A sovereign agentic website is a repository the owner controls. It holds:

  • The site, as plain files

    Static files, built once and served as they are.

  • Its content and its schema

    The structure of the site as files in open formats, not a format a vendor chose.

  • Instructions for the agents

    What the site is, how to change it, and what not to touch.

  • The behaviour policy

    What any agent working on the site may and may not do.

  • Checks and a deploy

    Checks that run before anything goes live, and a deploy that runs on any host.

Source: Sovereign Agentic Website.

Others can build on it

Because it is open, the pattern is also a base for other businesses: partners who sell and support it in their own country and language, agencies, newsrooms, councils. Nothing here is tied to the UK or to English. The agents write in the customer's language, the site is static and serves anywhere, and a partner invoices in their own currency under their own law.

In the partner model as drafted, the customer owns their site, their repository and their material, and the partner owns the relationship. Hosting, support and retainers belong to the partner. And a partner who takes the open code and does it alone is still a win: one more team running the technology, and one more place where agent behaviour policies are used for real.

The code and the policies are open source, so even we cannot lock anyone in.

The partner terms are a first draft, and no partner has signed yet. Source: Partners sell, we build, Sovereignty you can move and No lock-in.
