Where is your website, from 0 to 5?

Owning a website is the easy part. Moving it is where it fails. Sovereignty is not yes or no: it is how far you can move, on your terms. Five questions, one minute, and you know your level.

  1. Level 0, rented

    A hosted builder decides everything.

  2. Level 1, exportable

    Your words leave; the design stays.

  3. Level 2, a part you do not control

    Open software, held by one paid plugin, theme or agency component.

  4. Level 3, open, on one host

    Open code, tied to one host's special features.

  5. Level 4, portable

    Plain files, open tools, your domain at your registrar.

  6. Level 5, sovereign and proven

    Portable, and deployed on more than one host.

What you get

  • A team builds it

    An interview about what you want, then a team of agents makes five designs from your own photos and words. You pick one.

  • You own it

    A repository in your name with every change since the first, and, when you have one, your domain in your own name.

  • You change it by talking

    Your own AI changes the site when you ask, inside a behaviour policy that says what it may touch. Every change is a commit, checked before it goes live, and can be undone.

  • You can move it

    Plain files, no server code, no database, no cookies unless you add them. Any host that serves files can run it.

How it works, step by step

What it is built on

Sovereign Website is the top layer of an open stack, and the first one a florist, a bakery or a surf school can buy without knowing the others exist.

  1. Sovereign WebsiteA website built by agents and owned by the person it is for.
  2. AgentiCCellsAgentic teams, cells, installed for a customer, each with a job, a deliverable and a behaviour policy. Sovereign Website is one of its cells, its first mass-market product.
  3. RiskMandateBehaviour policies that say what agents may and may not do.
  4. SGitEncrypted, versioned vaults as an open standard, where work and its history live.

For larger agentic teams, for a company rather than a website, see agenticcells.ai.
