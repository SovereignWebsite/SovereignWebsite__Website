Five designs

Five designs for this home page. Design 1 was chosen.

Every site made this way starts with five designs, and its owner picks one. This site is made the same way: here are five complete directions for the home page of sovereignwebsite.ai, each with the same true content in a different order, voice and look.

Design 1, the plain promise, was chosen by Dinis Cruz on 20 November 2026, with its message “Your website. Built by agents, owned by you.” It is now the home page, and its look is the theme of the whole site. The other four stay here as the record of the choice.

The five directions

  • Design 1, chosen

    The plain promise

    “Your website. Built by agents, owned by you.”

    For
    A small business owner who wants a site and has never thought about owning one.
    Order
    The promise first, then four plain promises, the cost of renting, and the maturity model last.
    Voice
    Warm and direct, like a shopkeeper talking.
    Look
    Cream and terracotta, a large serif headline, the promotional prices as a ticket beside it.

    Open design 1, the plain promise

  • Design 2

    The question

    “Where is your website, from 0 to 5?”

    For
    Someone who already has a site and is not sure what they really own.
    Order
    The question first, the six levels as a scale, the five questions in full, then the answer and the prices.
    Voice
    Questions in the second person; it invites a check before it asks for anything.
    Look
    Cool and clinical, with the six level colours as a measuring scale.

    Open design 2, the question

  • Design 3

    Under your own roof

    “A website under your own roof.”

    For
    A European business that cares where its files, its name and its host are.
    Order
    The roof first, then three rooms: your files, your domain, your host; then who owns the platforms, the model and prices in pounds and euros.
    Voice
    Calm and civic, about place and control.
    Look
    A deep blue hero with a drawn roof over three rooms.

    Open design 3, under your own roof

  • Design 4

    Show, then own

    “Five designs. Pick one. It is yours.”

    For
    Someone who will choose with their eyes.
    Order
    The five designs first, as a gallery, then the six steps that lead to them, what you own, the model and the prices.
    Voice
    Short and confident: show, then own.
    Look
    A dark gallery wall of five page sketches, bold heavy type.

    Open design 4, show, then own

  • Design 5

    The open pattern

    “Websites built by agents and owned by the people they are for.”

    For
    Developers, partners and anyone who will use or build on the open pattern.
    Order
    The pattern first, then the repository, the stack, the seven principles, how to verify the claims, and the hosted option last.
    Voice
    Precise and technical, with every claim checkable.
    Look
    Monospace on graph paper, the repository drawn as a file tree.

How they were made

  • A design agent made all five from the brief in Designing sovereignwebsite.ai, which sets out five directions and six ways to say what Sovereign Website is. The sixth way, “Stop renting your website”, appears inside design 1 and on moving your site.
  • Every claim on them is already on this site or in the articles it links to, and every price carries its label.
  • They are plain HTML and CSS, like the rest of the site: no scripts, no images from anyone else. The pictures are drawn in CSS and SVG, because there are no real photographs or customer sites to show yet.
  • On a customer's site, the five are inspired by the sites the owner said they like: not copies, but the qualities they named. See how it works.
