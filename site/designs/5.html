The open pattern

Websites built by agents and owned by the people they are for.

Anyone can use the sovereign agentic website pattern without paying us, the way anyone can install WordPress. That is the point: a site you own should not depend on us existing.

Read the principles at sovereignwebsite.org The open pattern

A repository, not a platform

A sovereign agentic website is a repository the owner controls. Agentic is how the site is built and changed. Sovereign is what it is, whoever built it.

Source: Sovereign Agentic Website and What is a sovereign website.

  • repository/ owned by you
  • site plain files, built once and served as they are
  • content and schema open formats, not a format a vendor chose
  • CLAUDE.md what the site is, how to change it, what not to touch
  • behaviour policy what any agent may and may not do
  • checks and a deploy run before anything goes live, on any host

Seven principles, testable against any site

A sovereign website could be made by hand. A website is sovereign when:

  1. You own it. The site, its content and its history are in a repository in your name, and the domain is registered to you, at a registrar you chose.
  2. You can move it. It runs on any commodity host that can serve files, without changes.
  3. Nothing in the middle is proprietary. Every part it needs to build and run is open and commodity.
  4. Its content and schema are open. Words, data and their structure are in open formats you can read without the tool that made them.
  5. Anyone can change it. You, any developer, or your own AI, without asking the company that built it.
  6. What works on it is bounded. Agents and tools that change it work inside a behaviour policy that says what they may touch.
  7. It is proven, not claimed. It is deployed, regularly, on more than one host.

From them follows the maturity model, from level 0 to level 5:

  1. Level 0, rented

    A hosted builder decides everything.

  2. Level 1, exportable

    Your words leave; the design stays.

  3. Level 2, a part you do not control

    Open software, held by one paid plugin, theme or agency component.

  4. Level 3, open, on one host

    Open code, tied to one host's special features.

  5. Level 4, portable

    Plain files, open tools, your domain at your registrar.

  6. Level 5, sovereign and proven

    Portable, and deployed on more than one host.

Run the one-minute self-check

Do not take our word for it

  • The policy is public

    The SovereignWebsite organisation runs under an Agent Behaviour Policy that CI reads on every run. Anything it does not list is refused.

  • Every commit is signed

    Each agent signs with its own key and names itself and its session in Agent: and Claude-Session: lines. The public keys are in team/keys.json.

  • This page is what it says

    Plain HTML and CSS: no scripts, no forms, no cookies, no tracking, nothing loaded from anyone else.
