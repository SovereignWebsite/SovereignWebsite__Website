Questions

Is there a monthly fee?

No subscription from us. Hosting can be free: GitHub Pages serves a static site at no charge. Your domain is yours to pay for.

Can I pay on this site?

Not today. You ask through the Hub's start page, and we reply.

What happens after hand-over?

You change the site with your own AI (a Claude plan), edit the files yourself, or ask a partner.

Can I move it later?

Yes. Plain files, no server code, no database: any host that serves files can run it. See moving your site.

What does promotional mean?

These prices were set by Dinis Cruz on 25 November 2026 for the launch, and they can change at any time.

What if I need more?

Ask a partner. Partners may use the published ladder of £500, £1,500, £5,000 and £20,000, or set their own prices.
