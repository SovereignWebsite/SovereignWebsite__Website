1What it is

Tell a team of agents what you want. They build your site and hand it over. You keep the files and every change since the first, and you can move it to any host that serves files.

North Studio is a fictional architecture studio, made to show the five designs.

2The prices

As it is

£50

  • You send us the details.
  • We build your site and hand it over as it is.
  • After that you make any changes yourself.

With rounds of changes

£500

  • The same as £50.
  • We go back and forth with you a couple of times before hand-over.
  • Any further customisation we agree with you.

Promotional prices, set by Dinis Cruz on 25 November 2026; they can change at any time. Paid once, no subscription from us. You pay for your own domain if you want one; to edit the site yourself you need a Claude plan, or a partner. Every price, with its label.

3What you get

  • A team builds it

    An interview about what you want, then a team of agents builds your site from your own photos and words.

  • You keep the files

    Its own repository, with every change since the first. Plain files you can copy anywhere.

  • You change it by talking

    Your own AI changes the site when you ask, inside a behaviour policy that says what it may touch. Every change can be undone.

  • You can move it

    No server code, no database. Any host that serves files can run it.

4How it works

  1. Tell us about you

    Who you are, what you do, a link or two, and any photos you want used.

  2. We build it

    A team of agents makes your site from what you sent.

  3. It is handed over

    You get the site, and from then on you can change it yourself.
