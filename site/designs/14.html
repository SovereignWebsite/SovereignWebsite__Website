A story, and a fictional one

How a painter got a website she owns.

Ines Hart is fictional. Her story shows how the service works, step by step; she is not a customer.

Ines paints the sea, in oil and watercolour. Her work lived on a page inside a website builder: a monthly plan, a design she could not change much, and no way to take the site with her if she left.

She sent a short note, a few paintings and a link to her Instagram page. A team of agents read them and made five designs of the same site. She picked the one that gave the paintings the most room, asked for one change, and the site was handed over.

“I wanted a site I could keep.”

Ines Hart, a fictional painter

Now the files and every change since the first are hers. She changes the site by asking her own AI, and she can move it to any host that serves files.

The steps

  1. She asks with a note, photos and a link.
  2. Five designs of the same site.
  3. She picks one and asks for a change.
  4. It is hers: the files and their history.
