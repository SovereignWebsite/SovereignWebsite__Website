Trust

Do not take our word for it. Read the policy, check the signatures.

This site is built by a team of agents, the way the product is. What those agents may do is written down in public, and every change they make is signed and names the session that made it.

The behaviour policy

The SovereignWebsite organisation is run from one control repository, SovereignWebsite__Factory, under an Agent Behaviour Policy: policy/abp.json. CI reads it on every run. Anything it does not list is refused. In short:

  • Each agent may change only the paths listed for it, and only on the dev branch. Changes to the policy itself, and to the pipelines, take effect only when Dinis Cruz merges them.
  • Each pipeline may take only the actions listed for it. The one that manages repositories can create them and set them up; it can never delete, transfer, archive or make one private, change members, or weaken a ruleset.
  • The DNS pipeline can only add or update records named in the repository, in the project's own zones; it can never delete a record or a zone, or move a domain.
  • Every privilege is listed with its reason, for the GitHub App and the cloud role the pipelines use.
  • main moves only when Dinis Cruz merges a release. Agents push to dev. Rulesets forbid deleting or force-pushing either branch, and only Dinis, as the organisation's administrator, can bypass them on main.
  • No agent may print or commit a key or token, or name a private prospect. The rules require the tests, a policy check and a leak scan before every push.

Source: policy/abp.json and the factory's README. On behaviour policies in general: Designing sovereignwebsite.ai and A website with a team inside.

Signed commits

Every commit an agent makes is signed with its own key, as a git SSH signature, and carries two lines that say who made it: Agent:, the agent's name, and Claude-Session:, a link to the session it worked in. The public half of each key is published in team/keys.json, with its fingerprint, its role and the session that made it. Only the public half is there.

In the factory repository, the Checks pipeline verifies signatures and the policy's paths on every push. The commits to this site's own repository are signed the same way, and you can read them in its history on GitHub.

What this site does and does not do

  • No scripts

    Plain HTML and one stylesheet. The self-check works without JavaScript.

  • No cookies, no tracking

    Nothing to accept, nothing to put behind a banner, and no analytics of any kind on the page.

  • No forms

    The site collects nothing. Your answers to the self-check stay with you.

  • Nothing from anyone else

    No third-party fonts, images or scripts: your browser asks only this site for its pages.
