Prices

Paid once. Yours for good.

A platform charges every month because you stay. A sovereign agentic website is meant to be paid for once, with no subscription from us, for a site that is yours from the first day.

You cannot buy anything on this site today. The hosted service under this name does not exist yet. Every figure below is labelled with its status.

The prices, as published

  • Open pattern

    Free

    Use the pattern yourself without paying us, the way anyone can install WordPress. The pattern\'s own repository does not exist yet. About the open pattern.

  • Published anchor

    £500

    One-off, no subscription from us: the anchor price of the website cell, which this follows. The agents build the site, and on the hosted version it would be served on sovereignwebsite.site until you point your own domain at it.

  • Promotional prices

    £50 or £500

    Promotional, paid once. £50: you send us the details, a team of agents builds your site from your own words and photos, shows you five designs, and hands it over as it is; after that you make any changes yourself. £500: the same, and we also go back and forth with you a couple of times before hand-over; any further customisation we agree with you. The files and the history are yours, and so is the address if you bring your own domain, with no subscription from us. You pay for your own domain if you want one; to edit the site yourself you need a Claude plan, or a partner. How to start. Set by Dinis Cruz, 25 November 2026; it can change at any time.

Source: Sovereign Agentic Website, which says the prices follow the website cell's, The website cell and The sovereign website, where the £50 offer was proposed.

Planned: a Kickstarter campaign for a sovereign website appliance

This is a plan. Nothing has been submitted to Kickstarter, and the goal amount is not set. Kickstarter's review may ask for changes. If a campaign runs, it is all-or-nothing: if it does not reach its goal, nobody pays and nothing is built.

The plan is to launch Sovereign Website on Kickstarter as an appliance: a sovereign website on a device its owner holds. Either an Android tablet configured with the site, a way to change it by talking, monitoring of the live site, the AI app of the owner's choice and a backup to an encrypted vault; or a set of Raspberry Pis that serve the site from home and keep a full copy, while it stays on GitHub Pages or any static host too. The appliance's software is itself a plan.

  • Planned tier

    £50

    Supporter

    Your sovereign website, set up and handed over, with no hardware.

  • Planned tier

    £500

    Appliance

    Your website, and about £250 spent on hardware of your choice: a tablet, or two Raspberry Pi kits.

  • Planned tier

    £1,000

    Appliance Pro

    Your website, and about £500 spent on hardware of your choice: a tablet that can run AI models on the device, a European tablet, or more Raspberry Pi kits.

Planned add-ons, at any tier: your own app made from your site, about £500; your own product configured to run on the device, about £1,500. The promise is about what is spent on hardware, not a model number: the hardware is chosen after the campaign, at the best available for that amount at the time.

The UK is the planned base goal; Portugal the first stretch goal; then countries, and iOS, by backers' vote. In the plan, the site on its own, without a device, is sold directly on sovereignwebsite.ai, once there is something to sell.

Source: Launching the sovereign website appliance, with examples of what the hardware amounts buy in the UK today.

What else it costs to run

  • Hosting can be free. GitHub Pages serves a static site at no charge, and the files can go to any other web host.
  • Your own AI does the editing. You change the site with your own Claude, so you need a Claude plan to edit it yourself, or a partner to do it for you. The platforms bundle their editor; we do not.
  • Your domain is yours to pay for. You hold it at a registrar of your own; watch the renewal price. See owning your domain.
  • Help after hand-over is a partner's to sell. Hosting, support, changes and retainers belong to partners, at their own prices. We take no part in subscriptions. See partners.

Source: The website cell, The sovereign website and Partners sell, we build.

Renting against owning

On a platform you pay every month, for a site you cannot take with you. A year of Wix costs about £108 to £300 on its main plans; Squarespace starts from about $19 a month. When you cannot leave, the price is whatever the platform says: this year both Squarespace and Wix raised their prices.

Paid once, a sovereign agentic website is a repository you keep, on any host you choose.

Source: The sovereign website, No lock-in and Static against the platforms. Platform prices as those articles read them in November 2026; they change.
