Pricing

Paid once. Yours for good.

A platform charges every month because you stay. A sovereign agentic website is meant to be paid for once, with no subscription from us, for a site that is yours from the first day.

Nothing is sold yet. The hosted service is being set up. The figures below are the ones published so far, and you cannot buy anything on this site today.

The options, as published

  • Open pattern

    Free

    Use the pattern yourself without paying us, the way anyone can install WordPress. About the open pattern.

  • Hosted, anchor price

    £500

    One-off. No subscription from us. The agents build the site, and it is served on sovereignwebsite.site until you point your own domain at it.

  • Proposal only

    £50 or €50

    A launch offer that has been proposed, not decided: £50, and €50 on European sites. It is not on offer today.

Source: Sovereign Agentic Website, which says the prices follow the website cell's, and The sovereign website, where the launch offer was proposed.

What else it costs to run

  • Hosting can be free. GitHub Pages serves a static site with HTTPS, a custom domain included, at no charge, and the files can go to any other web host.
  • Your own AI does the editing. You change the site with your own Claude, so you need a Claude plan to edit it yourself, or a partner to do it for you. The platforms bundle their editor; we do not.
  • Your domain is yours to pay for. If you want your own name, you hold it at a registrar of your own. See Your own registrar for a comparison.
  • Help after hand-over is a partner's to sell. Hosting, support, changes and retainers belong to partners, at their own prices. We take no part in subscriptions.

Source: The website cell, The sovereign website and Partners sell, we build.

Renting against owning

On a platform you pay every month, forever, for a site you cannot take with you. When you cannot leave, the price is whatever the platform says: this year both Squarespace and Wix raised their prices.

Paid once, a sovereign agentic website is a repository you keep, on any host you choose.

Source: No lock-in and Static against the platforms.
