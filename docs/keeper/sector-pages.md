# Sector pages: structure and draft copy (not published; for review)

Rules: only true statements, no customer named or counted, a consented example only where listed in now.md, British English, no em dashes. Price line: "£50 or £500, promotional, paid once" (see pricing.html). Every page ends with one action: ask for yours by email (structured mailto for now).

## Template (one page per sector, at site/for/<sector>.html when the design is agreed)
1. Headline: what the page is for this person, in their words.
2. One line: who builds it and what they keep.
3. Three things you get (files and history yours, you choose where it runs, five designs).
4. What we need from you (a short list: your words, photos, links, which things you like).
5. The price, as on pricing.html, with the status label.
6. Example: only a consented one.
7. Ask for yours.

## Artist
Headline: Your work, on a website you own.
Line: Send us your pictures and your words. A team of agents builds your site, shows you five designs, and hands over the files and the history.
Needs: images (links or files), a few lines about you, links you want shown (an Instagram page, a shop).
Note: no example yet.

## Professional ("Profile")
Headline: A profile that is yours, not a page on someone else's platform.
Line: A profile page you own, built from your story, your work and your links. You can move it to any host.
Needs: your history in your own words (a CV is fine as a starting point), a photo, links.
Note: this is a page you own, with no platform fees from us; it is not a replacement for anyone's network. No example yet.

## Small business or tradesperson
Headline: A website for your business, built once.
Line: Say what you do, where, and how people reach you. We build it, you keep it.
Needs: a description, photos, contact details, opening hours or areas served.
Note: no example yet.

## Organisation or community group
Headline: A home for your group that you control.
Line: Chapters, events, people and news in plain files, in a repository in your name.
Needs: who you are, what you run, who may be named.
Example (consented): london.agentics-foundation.organisation (check the address and wording before use).

## Author
Headline: Your book, your page.
Line: A page for a book and its author, owned by the author.
Example: one-human-nine-agents.dinis.author (Dinis's own book; the address is http for now).

## Tools and demos
Headline: Show what your tool does.
Line: A small site per tool, with a live demo where it exists.
Examples: startup-grants.tool, whois.tool. Say "being built" for the Gmail and CRM tools, never "live".
